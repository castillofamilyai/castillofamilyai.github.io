Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Family HQ is a private tool used only by members of the Castillo family. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

Family HQ accesses the Castillo family's own Google account (Calendar, Gmail, and Drive) solely to help organize family schedules, reminders, and correspondence.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide this assistance to the family. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose. Google user data is not transferred to third parties except the AI service that processes requests on the family's behalf.

Contact

Questions: the family account's owner, via the support email listed on the app's Google sign-in screen.
